# Construction Accounting Video Study — September 2026

## Scope
This study supports the accounting-only Charismak mobile reset. The review window is 6 March–6 September 2026. It uses recent construction-accounting, contractor-finance, job-costing and construction-software training videos/webinars. Where YouTube captions/descriptions were available they were used; where a full transcript was not exposed, the published chapter/description and the provider's recorded-webinar notes were used. Full copyrighted transcripts are not reproduced here; notes are paraphrased.

## Weighted repeated lessons
The weights below represent how often a concept appeared materially across the 20 sessions/recordings, not a statistical claim about the entire industry.

| Priority | Repeated lesson | Weight | Product consequence |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Costs/revenue must be attributable to a job/project when relevant | Very high (~18/20) | Project is optional context for general accounting, but mandatory for direct construction job cost. |
| 2 | Cash is not profit; timing, WIP, commitments and retainage matter | Very high (~15/20) | Never label project cash balance as profit. |
| 3 | Budget/estimate, actual, committed and forecast are different numbers | Very high (~15/20) | Keep these fields/reports distinct. Do not overwrite one with another. |
| 4 | Capture costs at source with minimal friction | Very high (~14/20) | Mobile transaction entry must be fast; receipts/time/vendors are context, not barriers. |
| 5 | Review job-cost variance frequently, not only at closeout | High (~13/20) | Weekly/monthly project health should surface drift early. |
| 6 | Labour/time/payroll must flow to the correct job | High (~12/20) | Labour allocation is a later construction layer; avoid manual duplicate entry. |
| 7 | Cost codes must be lean enough that people use them correctly | High (~11/20) | Start with simple categories; advanced codes are optional. |
| 8 | Vendor/subcontract commitments matter before cash is paid | High (~10/20) | Commitments belong in later V1.x job control, separate from paid expenses. |
| 9 | WIP/percentage complete is essential for larger fixed-price construction | High (~10/20) | Add only after reliable budget + actual + progress exist. |
| 10 | Change orders require explicit state and financial effect | Medium-high (~9/20) | Never silently alter contract value or budget. |
| 11 | Retainage is not ordinary cash or revenue | Medium-high (~8/20) | Model separately when introduced. |
| 12 | Integrated systems beat duplicate data entry | Medium-high (~8/20) | Accounting ledger is source of truth; project views derive from it. |
| 13 | Historical actuals improve future pricing | Medium (~7/20) | Estimating can reconnect later, but must not block accounting. |
| 14 | Field adoption beats theoretical feature depth | Medium-high (~9/20) | Large readable text, few required fields, clear language. |

## Cross-check against the current Charismak APK
The current APK violates several high-weight lessons:

- Money is mostly read-only; the user cannot perform the basic accounting job of recording money in/out/transfer from the native Money screen.
- Estimate/BOQ occupies a primary tab even though accounting is now the product focus.
- Several dormant routes advertise or contain future schemas that do not exist in the active accounting database.
- Typography is too small for field/mobile use.
- The mobile flow is project-first; general/company transactions need a first-class path in the future architecture.
- Project cash position has previously risked being presented too close to profitability, which the research strongly rejects.
- Workspace/bootstrap errors can block the entire app and are not surfaced clearly enough.

## V1 accounting-only boundary
Keep now:
- sign in / workspace
- Home cash summary
- financial accounts
- money in / money out / transfer
- projects/jobs
- project cash received / spent / funding position
- recent activity
- simple categories
- basic cash-flow/category/project reports

Defer:
- estimating and BOQ
- procurement
- marketplace
- WIP/revenue recognition UI
- commitments and purchase orders
- retainage
- change-order commercial workflow
- approvals/team complexity beyond stable roles
- automatic bank-notification capture
- AI automation

The deferred items are important, but the repeated research supports adding them only after transaction capture and job-cost attribution are reliable.
